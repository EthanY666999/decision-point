The frozen train.main calls initial evaluate and then collect(step1) directly.
At step10, it similarly calls evaluate and then collect(step11) without another
sync_weights/reset between them. The model has not changed in either gap.
Evaluation therefore leaves executable prefix-cache state for the next training
collection. Evaluation-entry reset alone isolates evaluation from earlier cache
history but does not isolate later training from evaluation's cache history.

This is a possible numerical/execution-state effect. No validation labels,
rewards, trajectories or optimizer updates enter the next training batch through
this mechanism; there is no evidence here of validation-label leakage.
