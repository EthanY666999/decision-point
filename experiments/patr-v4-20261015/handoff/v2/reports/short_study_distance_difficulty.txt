解释边界：
- The four arms revisit the same 43 farther tasks; do not call 688 trajectories independent task evidence.
- All per-distance results are post-hoc descriptive subsets, some with few tasks; no new hypothesis-test claims or checkpoint selection.
- Random-policy values are exact policy expectations averaged across the same task subset, not an observed finite-sample score or proof of superiority.
- Pass4 averages per-task 1-(1-p_i)^4 for four independent random trajectories; applying the power to the overall average success is incorrect.
- Distance alone does not characterize map difficulty; size, holes and branching geometry also differ across strata.
- The primary results remain the original fixed step20 observations, not repeated final or a midpoint maximum.
