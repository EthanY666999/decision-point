## Sokoban implementation and acceptance

6×6, one box, deterministic orthogonal push-only actions, 10 turns, outcome reward solved=1 otherwise=0. Room generator is a documented local gym-sokoban-style implementation, not claimed to reproduce RAGEN's room distribution: border walls, independent 0.15 probability for internal walls, uniform distinct free player/box/goal cells, reject unless exact state-space BFS solves in 1–10 steps. No pulling, no step reward or reward shaping. Corner deadlocks are UCF FAIL classes but do not terminate or reward early.

Maps and seeds are unique; frozen 10,000/100/100/500 splits. First 1,000 training maps supply shortest BFS teachers, never validation/test. All 1,000 replay successfully and their action/eos masks match cumulative rollout encoding. Player+box anchors are used by GiGPO; UCF replay verifies both, not player location alone. Consequences are STAY / non-goal corner FAIL / GOAL / deterministic full state hash. Process progress is clipped 1 minus box-goal Manhattan over its initial value, as specified by taskbook 0.9. UCF beta=.25, rho_min=.03, F=2, target forks=2 stay unchanged.

## Recovery engineering

On an authorized UCF checkpoint retry, the controller is restored from the exact checkpoint-step snapshot. Later select, budget, controller and raw-leaf files are moved to a timestamped recovery archive with SHA256 receipts before deterministic replay. Missing or mismatched checkpoint snapshots are rejected before any mutation; controller history is never invented. CPU tests cover rollback, preserved evidence, exclusive-create replay, repeated reconciliation and missing-snapshot rejection. GPU end-to-end recovery has not yet been exercised. F02 source and process are unaffected.

A resumed checkpoint at a scheduled validation step (including 400) must have complete fixed-validation records for the exact tasks and four distinct lineages. Missing, partial or inconsistent records are archived with hashes and replayed using that checkpoint and the original validation seed. Already complete validation is reused; non-validation steps do not add evaluations. Tests cover this without using the test set or a GPU.
